## 0.1.0

First release.

- Shows one Trilium to-do list after sign-in, every unlock and every resume from standby, centred and on top.
- Add, finish, reopen and reorder items (mouse, keyboard, drag and drop); sub-items move with their parent.
- Keeps formatting, links, sub-items, task states and all other note content.
- Works offline from a saved copy and sends queued changes later; saves a Trilium revision before the first change each day.
- Encrypts the ETAPI token with the system's secure storage.
- Windows installer with autostart; Linux deb and AppImage with autostart and a systemd user wake service.
